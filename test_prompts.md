# SQL Agent Test Prompts

## Basic — Single Table
1. How many sales records are there?
2. What are the different product divisions?
3. What are the names of all the factories?
4. What is the sales target for each division?
5. How many units were sold in total?
6. What was the total sales revenue?

## Product Questions
7. What products are manufactured at each factory?
8. What is the unit price and unit cost of each product?
9. Which products have the highest unit cost?
10. Which products have the highest profit margin?

## Sales Analysis
11. Which product generated the most sales?
12. Which division generated the most sales?
13. Which division sold the most units?
14. What was the total gross profit?
15. What are the top 5 products by sales?
16. What are the top 5 products by units sold?

## Multi-Table Queries
17. What factory produces the product that generated the most sales?
18. How much revenue did each factory's products generate?
19. What are the top 5 products by sales, and which factories produce them?
20. Compare actual sales to the sales target for each division.
21. Which divisions exceeded their sales target?
22. Which factory generated the most sales?
23. What is the total sales and gross profit for each product division?

## Geographic Queries
24. Which states generated the most sales?
25. How much sales came from each city?
26. What are the top 10 ZIP codes by sales?
27. How much sales came from Michigan?
28. What is the population of the ZIP codes where our customers generated the most sales?

## Natural / Analytical Questions
29. How are we doing against our targets?
30. What are our best performing products?
31. Which factories are driving the most revenue?
32. Where are most of our customers located?
33. Which product division is performing the best?
34. Show me products that are selling for more than twice their production cost.
35. Which products have generated the most profit relative to their cost?
